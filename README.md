# NOFEE - free tools for selling a home

**NOFEE** (nofee.realestate) has free tools for selling a home. The tools are free and need no sign-up.

<p align="center"><a href="https://nofee.realestate/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme"><b>Calculate the commission on your sale price &rarr;</b></a><br>
<sub>Everything here is free.</sub></p>

## The tools
- **Commission calculator** - enter your sale price and see what an agent's commission costs and who pays it: Germany, Poland and other markets. Germany and Poland are sourced to the law; other markets are shown as typical ranges, marked as such. The calculation runs in your browser.
- **Selling costs** - Germany and Poland side by side: the commission, which side pays it, and what the law requires.
- **The NOFEE Index** - official house price statistics from government sources; each figure names its official source and its terms of use, and the method is public.

## Please note
Estimates and official statistics only: not an offer, not a valuation, and not legal, tax or financial advice.
